<section class="page-hero"><span class="eyebrow">Official policy</span><h1>Privacy Policy</h1><p>Effective September 15, 2026. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the UNJAM mobile application.</p></section>
<article class="doc">
  <p><strong>UNJAM respects your privacy.</strong> UNJAM does not require you to create an account to play the core games. Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.</p>

  <h2>Information We Collect</h2>
  <p>UNJAM may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, billing, or related app functionality. Those services may automatically process limited technical information such as device identifiers, app interactions, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, crash data, and purchase-related information, depending on the services enabled in the version of the app you are using and your device settings.</p>
  <p>UNJAM does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, private messages, contacts, photos, precise location, health information, or financial account credentials through normal gameplay.</p>

  <h2>Advertising</h2>
  <p>UNJAM may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements. Advertising providers may process device and advertising information in order to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable law.</p>
  <p>Where required, consent or privacy choices may be presented before personalized advertising is used.</p>

  <h2>In-App Purchases</h2>
  <p>If in-app purchases are enabled, transactions are processed by Google Play. UNJAM does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Purchase tokens may be sent to a Supabase Edge Function for validation with Google Play; the server ledger stores a SHA-256 fingerprint and purchase-claim metadata rather than the reusable raw Play purchase token.</p>

  <h2>Local Game Data</h2>
  <p>Game progress, settings, achievements, currencies, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete locally stored progress unless a cloud or backup feature is available in the version you are using.</p>

  <h2>Third-Party Services</h2>
  <p>UNJAM may use services provided by third parties such as Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, analytics providers, and crash-reporting tools. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and terms.</p>
  <p>For information about Google's privacy practices, visit <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</p>

  <h2>Data Sharing</h2>
  <p>UNJAM does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers only as needed to provide app functionality, advertising, analytics, billing, security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, or to comply with legal obligations.</p>

  <h2>Data Retention</h2>
  <p>Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is deleted, cleared, or removed with the app, subject to any backup or cloud features that may be enabled. Third-party service providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.</p>

  <h2>Data Deletion</h2>
  <p>You can remove locally stored UNJAM data by using available in-app reset options, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. For requests concerning data handled directly by a third-party provider, you may also need to use that provider's privacy or account controls.</p>

  <h2>Children's Privacy</h2>
  <p>UNJAM is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If features, advertising, or distribution settings are changed for audiences that include children, UNJAM will use appropriate child-directed settings and compliance measures where required.</p>

  <h2>Security</h2>
  <p>Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information associated with the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.</p>

  <h2>Your Choices</h2>
  <p>Depending on your device, region, and the services enabled in the app, you may be able to control advertising personalization, app permissions, diagnostics, notifications, and other privacy-related settings through Android, Google Play, or in-app controls.</p>

  <h2>Changes to This Policy</h2>
  <p>This Privacy Policy may be updated when UNJAM's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.</p>

  <h2>Contact</h2>
  <p>For privacy questions, data-related requests, or support, use the <a href="support.html">UNJAM support page</a> or the <a href="https://github.com/eghosa001/unjam/issues">public GitHub issue tracker</a>.</p>

  <h2>Developer</h2>
  <p>UNJAM is published under the developer account responsible for the Android application with package name <code>com.eghosa.unjam</code>.</p>
