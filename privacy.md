# Privacy Policy for UNJAM

**Effective date: September 15, 2026**

UNJAM respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the UNJAM mobile application.

## Information We Collect

UNJAM does not require you to create an account to play the core games. Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

UNJAM may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, billing, or related app functionality. Those services may automatically process limited technical information such as device identifiers, app interactions, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, crash data, and purchase-related information, depending on the services enabled in the version of the app you are using and your device settings.

UNJAM does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, private messages, contacts, photos, precise location, health information, or financial account credentials through normal gameplay.

## Advertising

UNJAM may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements. Advertising providers may process device and advertising information in order to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Where required, consent or privacy choices may be presented before personalized advertising is used.

## In-App Purchases

If in-app purchases are enabled, transactions are processed by Google Play. UNJAM does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Google may provide transaction status or purchase-token information needed to validate purchases and provide purchased content.

## Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, achievements, currencies, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete locally stored progress unless a cloud or backup feature is available in the version you are using.

## Third-Party Services

UNJAM may use services provided by third parties such as Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, analytics providers, and crash-reporting tools. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

For information about Google's privacy practices, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Sharing

UNJAM does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers only as needed to provide app functionality, advertising, analytics, billing, security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, or to comply with legal obligations.

## Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is deleted, cleared, or removed with the app, subject to any backup or cloud features that may be enabled. Third-party service providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

## Data Deletion

You can remove locally stored UNJAM data by using available in-app reset options, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. For requests concerning data handled directly by a third-party provider, you may also need to use that provider's privacy or account controls.

## Children's Privacy

UNJAM is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If features, advertising, or distribution settings are changed for audiences that include children, UNJAM will use appropriate child-directed settings and compliance measures where required.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information associated with the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Your Choices

Depending on your device, region, and the services enabled in the app, you may be able to control advertising personalization, app permissions, diagnostics, notifications, and other privacy-related settings through Android, Google Play, or in-app controls.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when UNJAM's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
