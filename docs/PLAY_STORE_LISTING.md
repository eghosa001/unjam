## Full description

UNJAM brings three distinct puzzle games together in one polished collection.

### Rescue Rush

Clear directional paths, release chains and guide each rescue to the open exit. Levels introduce new obstacles and increasingly demanding layouts as you progress.

### Water Sort

Sort colourful liquid between tubes until every colour is grouped correctly. Plan each pour, use undo or hints when needed, and aim for efficient solutions.

### Block Puzzle

Place shapes on an 8x8 board, create complete rows and columns, and keep enough space open for the next set of pieces. Smooth drag placement and clear visual feedback keep the focus on the puzzle.

### One shared journey

Switch between all three games from the UNJAM home screen. Each mode has its own progression, worlds, stars and large campaign, with daily challenges and collection rewards bringing the experience together.

### Player comfort

UNJAM includes sound, music, haptics, dark and light appearance options, reduced motion and large touch targets. Progress is saved automatically on your device.
