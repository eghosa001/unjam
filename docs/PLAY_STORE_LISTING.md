TITLE
UNJAM

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Three polished puzzle games in one app: rescue, sort colours, and clear blocks.

FULL DESCRIPTION
UNJAM brings three distinct puzzle games together in one polished collection.

RESCUE RUSH
Clear paths, move blockers and guide the rescue piece to safety. Plan efficient routes, build chains and solve increasingly demanding boards.

WATER SORT
Pour coloured liquid between tubes until every colour is sorted. Clean moves, readable animations and progressively harder layouts keep each puzzle satisfying.

BLOCK PUZZLE
Drag shapes onto an 8×8 board, complete rows and columns, and keep enough space open for the next set of pieces.

WHY UNJAM
• Three complete puzzle modes in one app
• Thousands of deterministic levels and progression goals
• Daily challenges and rewards
• Hints, undo and retry tools
• Dark and light appearance options
• Sound, music, haptics and reduced-motion settings
• Designed for portrait touch play

Progress is saved automatically so you can return to your current run at any time.
