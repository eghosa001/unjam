UNJAM Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 September 2026

UNJAM is a puzzle game. Core game progress is stored locally on the device. UNJAM may use Google Play Billing to process digital purchases and Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded and interstitial advertising. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are not stored by UNJAM.

Data used by third parties

Google services may process device identifiers, advertising information, purchase tokens, diagnostics, approximate location derived from network information, and interaction data as described in Google's policies and the Google Play Data Safety disclosures for the published app.

Consent

Where required, advertising consent is requested before advertising is loaded. Users can revisit privacy choices from the app's Privacy Options control.

Children

The production store listing must declare the app's intended audience accurately. UNJAM does not enable personalized advertising where applicable law or Google Play Families policies prohibit it.

Retention and security

Game progress is stored locally. Purchase entitlements can be restored through Google Play. Purchase verification tokens are sent to a Supabase Edge Function to validate purchases with Google Play and prevent duplicate grants. The server ledger stores a SHA-256 fingerprint and purchase-claim metadata rather than the reusable raw Play purchase token.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the developer contact published on the UNJAM Google Play listing.
